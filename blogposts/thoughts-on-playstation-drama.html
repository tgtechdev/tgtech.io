My thoughts on the PlayStation drama!

So the past few months, Sony has made some poor decisions, including getting rid of discs entirely. Out of nowhere, they completely terminated the contract with Hideo Kojima, the guy who created Metal Gear Solid and Death Stranding. The US tariff controversy has also added more fuel to the fire as well as age restriction for playing with friends and messaging them.

Getting rid of discs: Sony getting rid of discs has made it so there is no third party and that you HAVE to pay their price. And Sony has stated any digital games that you buy, you DO NOT own them. Imagine losing your entire catalogue of games?

Hideo kojima partnership: One of the outright idiotic mistakes Sony has made it that they have cut ties and terminated the contract with the guy who literally defined a genre with HIS games. Right after Sony dropped him, Microsoft’s Xbox division made a partnership with him and made it so Sony has lost the partnership with Hideo Kojima for the foreseeable future.

The US government gave Sony 508 million USD back after the tariffs set by the president. During the increased tariffs, Sony users and players essentially paid higher prices during this time, so they are now demanding some sort of compensation back. Of course, refunding half a billion USD to millions of users would be extremely time consuming and tedious. So they could probably do what Nintendo did and just lower the prices on consoles, games and memberships for the same amount of time the tariffs were affecting people. But unlike Nintendo - they haven't. But then again Sony isn’t inclined or isn't responsible for people having to pay that price for their services.
