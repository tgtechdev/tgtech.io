Thoughts on the GTA 6 hack

A week ago, GTA 6 was hacked and some cutscenes were leaked along with some gameplay. Of course, most people like to see some aspects of the game before its release because of sheer anticipation, but with leaks like this can be devastating for games and game developers.

But with the release of the new extended look on Netflix and YouTube, a lot of the doubt from all the players has somewhat disappeared. There are still a few people nit picking some things from the video like the animations from Jason.

With the release of the extended version loads of screenshots and information has been released on bigger gaming sites. Overall, I think what rockstar has done has put most doubters and leakers to rest with the new content and information.
